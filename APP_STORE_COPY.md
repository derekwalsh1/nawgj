## App Description (4000 character limit)

**NAWGJ Expense Tracker** is the essential tool for USA Gymnastics judges to manage meet assignments, track expenses, calculate fees, and generate professional invoices. Designed specifically for NAWGJ (National Association of Women's Gymnastics Judges) members, this app streamlines the entire judging workflow from meet creation to final payment tracking.

**KEY FEATURES**

📋 **Meet Management**
• Create and organize gymnastics meets with detailed schedules
• Track multiple meet days with start/end times and breaks
• Support for all competition levels: Levels 4-10, Xcel programs, and NGA
• Automatic billable hours calculation with break time deductions
• Meet referee designation with additional fee tracking

👥 **Judge Roster**
• Maintain your complete judge contact list
• Support for all NAWGJ certification levels with automatic rate assignments
• Quick judge selection when creating meets
• Import/export judges for easy sharing

💰 **Expense Tracking**
• Comprehensive expense categories: Mileage, Lodging, Airfare, Meals, Parking, Transportation, Tolls/Bridges, and Other
• Automatic mileage reimbursement calculation using current federal rates (2016-2026)
• Hotel accommodation tracking with per-night rates and single-room requests
• Receipt and W-9 status tracking
• Payment status management

💵 **Fee Calculation**
• Automatic fee calculation based on judge level and hours worked
• Support for custom hourly rates when needed
• Meet referee fee tracking ($200 standard)
• Exclude specific days from billing when necessary
• Real-time totals for fees, expenses, and overall meet costs

📊 **Professional Invoicing**
• Generate detailed PDF invoices for individual judges
• Comprehensive meet reports with all judges, fees, and expenses
• Meet summary with total hours, costs, and judge breakdown
• Checklist report for tracking W-9s, receipts, and payments
• Daily fee breakdowns by judge and session
• Meet day details with hours and judges per session

📤 **Import/Export**
• Export meets and judges to JSON format
• Share invoices and reports via email, Messages, AirDrop, or cloud storage
• Import meet and judge data for backup or sharing between devices
• Secure data storage on your device

📱 **Professional Design**
• Clean, intuitive interface designed for busy judges
• Quick access to all meet information
• Easy navigation between meets, judges, and expenses
• Dark mode support for comfortable viewing in any environment

**PERFECT FOR:**
• NAWGJ certified judges managing multiple meets
• Meet directors tracking judge payments
• Gymnastics clubs organizing invitational competitions
• Anyone needing professional expense and fee tracking

**DATA SECURITY**
Your data stays on your device. We don't collect, store, or share any of your meet or expense information. Import/export features give you full control over your data.

**SUPPORTED JUDGE LEVELS:**
• Levels 4-5, 6-8, 4-8
• Level 9, Level 10
• National, Brevet
• NGA: Local, State, Regional, National, Elite

**AUTOMATIC MILEAGE RATES:**
The app automatically uses the correct IRS mileage reimbursement rate based on the meet date, with rates from 2016-2026 built in, including the latest 2026 rate of $0.725/mile.

Make your judging assignments more organized and professional with NAWGJ Expense Tracker. Download now and simplify your meet management!
