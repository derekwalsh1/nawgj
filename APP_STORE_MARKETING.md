# App Store Assets

## App Store Badge

Once your app is approved, add this badge to your README.md:

### Markdown
```markdown
[![Download on the App Store](https://developer.apple.com/assets/elements/badges/download-on-the-app-store.svg)](https://apps.apple.com/app/idYOUR_APP_ID)
```

### HTML
```html
<a href="https://apps.apple.com/app/idYOUR_APP_ID">
  <img src="https://developer.apple.com/assets/elements/badges/download-on-the-app-store.svg" alt="Download on the App Store" height="50">
</a>
```

**Note:** Replace `YOUR_APP_ID` with your actual App Store ID (found in App Store Connect after app is created)

## QR Code for App Store

After approval, generate a QR code:

1. Go to: https://qr-code-generator.com
2. Enter your App Store URL: `https://apps.apple.com/app/idYOUR_APP_ID`
3. Download PNG
4. Add to your marketing materials

## FAQ for App Store Description (If Needed)

**Q: Does this work for all gymnastics programs?**
A: Yes! The app supports USAG Women's levels 4-10, Xcel (Bronze through Platinum), and NGA levels (Local through Elite).

**Q: Do I need an internet connection?**
A: No! All features work completely offline. Your data is stored on your device.

**Q: Can I share invoices with meet directors?**
A: Absolutely! Tap the share button on any invoice or report to send via email, Messages, AirDrop, or save to Files.

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes. All data stays on your device. We don't collect, store, or transmit any information.

**Q: Can I export my data?**
A: Yes! Export meets and judges to JSON format for backup or transfer between devices.

**Q: Does it track mileage automatically?**
A: The app uses the correct federal mileage rate based on your meet date. Just enter the miles driven and it calculates the reimbursement.

**Q: Can I customize judge rates?**
A: Each meet uses the standard rates for each judge level, but you can override rates per day if needed.
