# Privacy Policy for NAWGJ Expense Tracker

**Last Updated: February 16, 2026**

## Overview

NAWGJ Expense Tracker ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our iOS application.

## Information Collection and Use

**We do not collect any personal information.**

### Data Storage

All data created and managed within the App is stored locally on your iOS device. This includes:
- Meet information (names, dates, locations, descriptions)
- Judge information (names, certification levels)
- Expense data (amounts, categories, notes)
- Fee calculations
- Generated PDF documents

### Data We Do NOT Collect

The App does not collect, transmit, or store any of the following:
- Personal identification information
- Contact information
- Location data
- Usage analytics
- Crash reports
- Device identifiers
- Advertising identifiers

### Data You Control

You have complete control over your data:
- **Local Storage:** All data is stored exclusively on your device
- **Export:** You can export your data in JSON format at any time
- **Import:** You can import data from JSON files
- **Delete:** You can delete any meet, judge, or expense at any time
- **No Cloud Storage:** We do not sync or backup your data to any cloud service

### Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising networks.

### Data Sharing

The App allows you to share generated PDF invoices and reports using iOS's native share functionality. When you share:
- You control where and with whom the PDF is shared
- Sharing is done through standard iOS mechanisms (Mail, Messages, AirDrop, Files, etc.)
- No data is sent to our servers
- We have no access to shared documents

### Import/Export

The App supports importing and exporting data in JSON format:
- Exports are controlled entirely by you
- Exported files contain only the data you choose to export
- No data is transmitted to our servers during import/export
- Files are handled using standard iOS file management

### Security

Your data security is important to us:
- All data is stored using iOS's secure file system
- No network connections are made by the App
- No data leaves your device unless you explicitly share or export it
- We follow Apple's security best practices

### Children's Privacy

The App is rated 4+ and does not collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

### Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted in the App and on our GitHub repository. Your continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Since we don't collect any data, there is no data to:
- Access
- Correct
- Delete
- Transfer
- Object to processing

All data is yours and remains on your device.

### Compliance

This App complies with:
- Apple's App Store Review Guidelines
- iOS data protection requirements
- General best practices for user privacy

### Summary

**What We Collect:** Nothing  
**What We Store:** Nothing (except on your device)  
**What We Share:** Nothing  
**What We Track:** Nothing  

Your privacy is protected because we simply don't collect, store, or transmit any of your information.
